Why EarthPulse?

NASA publishes huge amounts of Earth data, but raw time series are hard to read and easy to misread. EarthPulse fetches the data for you, tests whether a change is real or just noise, and explains it in plain words.

How it works

  1. Get NASA dataWe request your place and years from NASA POWER.
  2. Validate dataMissing values are counted, never guessed.
  3. Detect trendIs the series going up, down, or neither?
  4. Measure changeHow much per year, and in total?
  5. Test significanceCould random variation explain it?
  6. Explain resultA plain-language summary, no invented causes.

Technical detail: data come from the NASA POWER monthly API (annual means, -999 fill values removed). Our calculations, not NASA's: Mann-Kendall test (tie-corrected, two-sided, α = 0.05) and Sen's slope. The explanation uses fixed templates, not AI. Limitations: annual means can be autocorrelated, which may overstate significance; POWER blends satellite and model data rather than station records; a trend alone does not show its cause.
